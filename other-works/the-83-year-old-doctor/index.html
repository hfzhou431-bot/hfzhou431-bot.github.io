THE 83-YEAR-OLD “YOUNG” DOCTOR | 83岁的“小”医生

Micro-documentary | 2023 | Hunan Daily / New Hunan

About the Project

Produced for Hunan Daily's New Hunan platform, The 83-Year-Old Doctor documents Wu Jinshu, an 83-year-old surgeon who continues to devote himself to medicine.

Through everyday moments of work, conversation, and reflection, the film offers a portrait of professional dedication, care, and the passing on of medical knowledge across generations.
