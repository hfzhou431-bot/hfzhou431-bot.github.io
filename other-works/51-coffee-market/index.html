51 COFFEE MARKET / 2025

Art Event | Mango Art Museum

About the Project

Held at Mango Art Museum, the 51 Coffee Market brought together coffee culture, creative brands, and public engagement within a contemporary art setting.

As part of the museum's project team, I contributed to the planning and execution of the three-day event, coordinating with external partners, supporting on-site operations, and helping deliver the festival.
