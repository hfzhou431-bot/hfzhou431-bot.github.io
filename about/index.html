I work across art, performance, and technology.

My background began in dance choreography, where I became interested in how movement communicates ideas and experiences. This curiosity gradually led me towards moving images, interactive media, and digital technologies.

I studied Creative Media at City University of Hong Kong and am currently pursuing an MPhil in Digital Humanities at the University of Cambridge.

Across different mediums, I remain interested in how technology changes the ways we create, experience, and understand art.
