CONVEX 凸

We are each unique, like works of art shaped by life. Even when marked by unexpected colours and imperfections, these traces become part of our individual aesthetic identities.

Through dance and moving images, the film explores self-acceptance, individuality, and the beauty of imperfection. It invites us to embrace life's irregularities and find harmony with ourselves and others through movement.
