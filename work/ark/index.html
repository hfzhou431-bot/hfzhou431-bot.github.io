Ark 方舟

Adrift in the boundless expanse, a fragment of land bears the weight of countless lives.

Tides ebb and flow; encounters dissolve into departures. Within a shared boundary, separate lives intersect, pull, and intertwine, seeking a delicate balance through silent tensions. The contours of individuality begin to blur, as breaths and pulses merge into a common rhythm.

The island drifts, and all life remains entwined. Perhaps the ark carries us towards shores that none could reach alone.
