KUI 夔

Inspired by the Kui Dragon motif found in ancient Chinese bronze vessels, Kui explores the transformation of traditional decorative imagery into embodied movement.

Drawing on the distinctive qualities of the motif, including its simplicity, strength, mystery, and intricate forms, the choreography translates static patterns into a physical vocabulary. Through the interplay of three performers, the work reconstructs the mythical Kui Dragon through abstract bodily forms, evoking the intricate structure and enigmatic presence of the original bronze ornamentation.
