After the Spring Equinox 春分之后

After the spring equinox, life begins to emerge. If all things possess a spirit, even the soil, the cradle of life, begins to stir and soften with the first signs of awakening.

Inspired by the ancient Chinese philosophy of "One gives birth to Two, Two gives birth to Three, and Three gives birth to all things," the work explores the emergence, growth, and interconnectedness of life through movement.

As the earth awakens, a new journey of life begins.
