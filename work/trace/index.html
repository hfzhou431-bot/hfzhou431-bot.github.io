TRACE | 溯

Contemporary Dance | Solo | 2023

Time does not flow in a straight line. What has been carried away leaves traces within the body.

Through repetition, retreat, and subtle shifts in movement, the body follows fragments of memory towards an elusive beginning. Yet with each return, the point of origin grows increasingly unfamiliar.

Trace explores the distance between memory and origin, where the act of returning becomes a process of transformation.
