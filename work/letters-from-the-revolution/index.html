Letters from the Revolution 红色家书

Letters from the Revolution is a Chinese historical dance drama inspired by real letters written by revolutionaries during times of war.

Through the personal stories of revolutionary figures and their families, the production explores love, separation, sacrifice, and the pursuit of freedom. Combining solo, duet, trio, and ensemble choreography, the work brings these historical narratives to life through movement, tracing the emotional connections between individuals, their loved ones, and a nation undergoing profound change.

Produced by the Dance School of Xi'an Conservatory of Music, the work was supported by the 2021 Shaanxi Provincial Arts Creation Funding Programme.
